Welcome to Hotel Le Grande, where hospitality awaits. Located in Ellisbridge, Ahmedabad, the hotel combines a convenient city location with comfortable interiors for business travellers, NRIs, medical tourists, citizens and other visitors.

With rooms, dining, business facilities and banquet services under one roof, Le Grande is designed to make your stay simple and comfortable.

From a warm welcome at reception to relaxed dining and well-equipped celebration spaces, every part of Le Grande is designed to make your Ahmedabad stay convenient and memorable.
